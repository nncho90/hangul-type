Progression Rule

Lv 1-4 · Tier 1One big shape, one horn, one prop. The player should read it instantly while learning the keyboard.
Lv 5-8 · Tier 2Still cute and readable, but adds tails, charms, or a second silhouette feature.
Lv 9-13 · Tier 3Medium complexity: masks, smoke, uneven movement, and stronger personality.
Lv 14-18 · Tier 4High detail: robes, lanterns, talismans, dive/tracker motion hooks.
Lv 19+ · Tier 5Most intricate normal enemy, with boss-adjacent silhouette and layered animation potential.
